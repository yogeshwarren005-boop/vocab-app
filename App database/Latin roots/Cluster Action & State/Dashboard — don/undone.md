> [!book] 📖 Definitions & Semantic Range
> 1. **Primary Definition (Lexical / Standard Consensus)**: Not done, finished, or completed; or unfastened; or ruined and brought to destruction.
> 2. **Secondary / Nuanced Definition (Specialized / Domain / Encyclopedic)**: In classical tragedy and Elizabethan drama, the lament 'I am undone' expresses catastrophic personal or dynastic ruin.
