> [!book] 📖 Definitions & Semantic Range
> 1. **Primary Definition (Lexical / Consensus)**: To oppress, harass, or pursue with persistent hostility and cruelty, especially for religion or race.
> 2. **Secondary / Nuanced Definition (Contextual / Classical)**: To annoy, pester, or trouble persistently with importunate demands.
